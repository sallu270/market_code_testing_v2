
Main tables:

### ticks

Stores each received market tick with exchange and local receipt timestamps,
LTP, volume, open interest, bid, ask and the source message.

### candles_1m

Stores local one-minute OHLC candles, volume change, open interest, tick count
and whether the candle is closed.

## Candle behaviour

- Candles align to exact one-minute Unix timestamp boundaries.
- Exchange timestamp is used when Upstox provides it.
- A candle closes when a tick for a later minute arrives.
- Housekeeping also closes a stale candle after the minute changes.
- Late ticks from an older minute are ignored by the in-memory builder.
- No artificial candle is generated for a minute with no ticks.
- India has no daylight-saving-time clock change; the dashboard explicitly
  displays Asia/Kolkata time.
- Index feeds may not contain meaningful traded volume. Futures and options are
  better sources for volume and open interest.
