1. System Architecture & Dual-Path Design
The automated trading engine operates on a Dual-Path Execution Model designed to capture both the immediate opening impulse at 09:15:00 and sustained institutional accumulation starting at 09:18:00 when the exchange releases fresh Open Interest (OI) feeds.
Path 1 (09:15:00 First-Tick Entry): Executes immediately on market open for instruments identified on yesterday's 3:15 PM EOD Watchlist (ranked ★★★ or above). Requires spot gap alignment (<= 0.3%) and triggers on the very first positive tick above the stored reference LTP.
Path 2 (09:18:00 All-Day OI Buildup Matrix): Runs from the first fresh OI broadcast through 14:45:00. Evaluates the 16-condition option buildup matrix combined with a strict Futures Direction Gate and consecutive confirmation filters.
Slippage & Fill Reality: All paper and live entries are filled strictly at the live Ask price; all exits are executed strictly at the live Bid price. Fills are never simulated at previous close, midpoint, or LTP.
Multi-Index & Stock Isolation: Each instrument (NIFTY, BANK NIFTY, SENSEX, and each screened stock) runs an independent state machine with dedicated cache, risk limits, and trade counters.

Tightest-Spread ITM Strike Selection
Rather than arbitrarily selecting an arbitrary strike, the bot evaluates the 4 locked In-The-Money (ITM) candidates and selects the contract possessing the tightest bid-ask spread %: Spread % = ((Ask - Bid) / Ask) * 100. This guarantees maximum liquidity, tightest fills, and lowest execution friction.

4. Entry Mechanism 1: 09:15 First-Tick Strategy (Path 1)
Path 1 captures the opening impulse immediately at 09:15:00 without waiting for OI updates. It requires pre-qualification from yesterday's 3:15 PM EOD Watchlist.
Watchlist Gate: Only instruments ranked ★★★ or ★★★★ on yesterday's 3:15 PM Watchlist are permitted to enter at 09:15:00.
The 0.3% Gap Filter: If the index or stock opens gapped AGAINST yesterday's watchlist direction by more than 0.3%, the trade is aborted immediately. For Bullish watchlist, gap down cannot exceed -0.3%; for Bearish watchlist, gap up cannot exceed +0.3%.
Bullish Entry Trigger (BUY_CE): Spot opens at or above previous close. On the very first tick received after 09:15:00 where contract LTP > Reference LTP (Snapshot / Prev Close) on the tightest-spread ITM CE, enter BUY_CE at live Ask.
Bearish Entry Trigger (BUY_PE): Spot opens at or below previous close. On the very first tick received after 09:15:00 where contract LTP > Reference LTP on the tightest-spread ITM PE (or Stock spot ticks below open price), enter BUY_PE at live Ask.
Strict Daily Limit: Exactly 1 First-Tick entry is allowed per instrument per day. Subsequent entries must originate from Path 2.
09:45 Time Cutoff: If the 09:15 entry reaches neither the 25% Stop-Loss nor the 40% Target by 09:45:00, the bot forcefully exits the position at the live market bid.
5. Entry Mechanism 2: 09:18 OI Buildup Strategy (Path 2)
Starting at 09:18:00, the exchange broadcasts fresh intraday Open Interest. Every incoming OI cycle triggers a complete re-evaluation of the ATM ±1 option chain against the 09:15:00 Baseline Snapshot.
Buildup Classification Rules
To eliminate false signals from market micro-noise, the bot applies a strict ±0.5% Price Deadband and a ±0.1% OI Threshold:
Long Buildup (LB): Price Change >= +0.5% AND OI Change >= +0.1% (Bullish buyers accumulating contracts).
Short Buildup (SB): Price Change <= -0.5% AND OI Change >= +0.1% (Bearish writers adding short exposure).
Short Covering (SC): Price Change >= +0.5% AND OI Change <= -0.1% (Sellers forced to cover; explosive upside).
Long Unwinding (LU): Price Change <= -0.5% AND OI Change <= -0.1% (Bulls closing out long positions; downward drift).
FLAT / Deadband: Any price movement between -0.5% and +0.5% is treated as FLAT to prevent false triggers during sideways chop.

The 2 Consecutive OI Updates Requirement
A single OI spike can be caused by temporary order placing or cancellations. To ensure institutional commitment, a valid signal must hold across 2 consecutive OI updates from the exchange before any order is sent to the market. If the signal flips or drops on the second update, the entry is canceled.
6. The Futures Direction Gate & SENSEX Protocol
Option chains reveal where writers are active, but futures OI provides the purest read of institutional directional conviction without Greek distortions (theta decay and implied volatility spikes).
The Directional Gate Mechanism
Before executing any trade generated by the 16-condition matrix, the bot queries the underlying current-month futures contract:
CE Buy Validation: Futures MUST be in Long Buildup (LB) or Short Covering (SC). If Futures is in Short Buildup (SB) or Long Unwinding (LU), the trade is BLOCKED immediately with reason: Opposing Futures.
PE Buy Validation: Futures MUST be in Short Buildup (SB) or Long Unwinding (LU). If Futures is in Long Buildup (LB) or Short Covering (SC), the trade is BLOCKED immediately with reason: Opposing Futures.

6A.1 Why Change-in-OI PCR, and not only Total OI PCR
1.  Total OI PCR (ΣPE OI ÷ ΣCE OI) includes all positions carried from previous sessions. It moves slowly and reacts late. It is displayed for context only.
2.  Change-in-OI PCR (dOI_PCR) uses only the OI added or removed today since 09:15:00. It shows fresh positioning and is the slow confirming input.
3.  Volume PCR (VOL_PCR) shows where trading activity is flowing right now. It is the fast, leading input.
4.  Core principle: sudden sharp moves and gamma blasts typically begin when the fast ratio (rolling Volume PCR) breaks sharply away from its normal level while the slow ratio (OI-based) has not yet moved. They are then confirmed when writers on the losing side start covering (their OI falls while volume stays high).
5.  Volume alone cannot tell a buyer from a writer. Therefore every flow decision is cross-checked against premium direction using the existing LB / SB / SC / LU buildup classification of Section 5.
6A.2 Flow Monitoring Basket (separate from the 8 locked trading contracts)
Indices: nearest (current) expiry series of each index, ATM ± 5 strikes (11 CE + 11 PE). Expiry is read from the contract master; weekday is never hardcoded.
NIFTY 50 Stocks: current-month series, ATM ± 3 strikes (7 CE + 7 PE). When the current month has ≤ 1 trading day left, the flow basket uses the next-month series (except inside the Stock Gamma module).
Baseline: the basket is fixed at 09:15:00 together with the existing Baseline Snapshot (LTP, OI, Volume of every basket contract).
Re-centring: only if spot stays ≥ 2 strike steps away from the basket ATM for ≥ 60 seconds. On re-centring, rebuild rolling windows and enter a 3-minute WARM_UP state in which no new flow signal is generated.
Lottery-ticket exclusion: contracts with LTP below min_premium (Index ₹3, Stock ₹1) are excluded from the PCR volume sums, but they feed the separate OTM_VSR gamma metric.

6A.4 Data Hygiene & Anti-Noise Guards (mandatory)
1.  Minimum denominator: VOL_PCR_5M = NULL if either side's 5-minute basket volume < min_window_vol (Index: 200 lots; Stock: 20 lots). NULL means no flow signal.
2.  Clamping: all PCR ratios are clamped to the range 0.10 – 10.0 before use.
3.  Warm-up: no VOL_PCR_5M-based signal before 09:20:00. Until 15 minutes of data exist, VOL_PCR_BASE = VOL_PCR_DAY.
4.  OI freshness: OI-based fields are recomputed only when a new OI timestamp arrives. If the OI timestamp is unchanged for > oi_stale_sec (300 sec), set OI_STALE = TRUE: block Path 3, Gamma entries and every CONFIRMED state.
5.  Block-print guard: if a single strike supplies > 50% of its side's window volume while its premium moves < 1%, tag BLOCK_PRINT (probable hedge / spread). The trigger then needs one additional confirming bar.
6.  Spread guard (Stocks): any basket contract with Spread % > 3% is excluded from the sums.
7.  Persistence: every IGNITION trigger must hold on 2 consecutive closed 1-minute bars (mirrors the 2-Consecutive OI Updates rule).
8.  Feed watchdog: the existing 60-second STALE_FEED rule also freezes FLOW_STATE at its last value and blocks flow-based entries.

6A.6 Category A – Indices (NIFTY 50, BANK NIFTY, SENSEX)
A1. Path 1 (09:15 First-Tick) – Opening Flow Abort
1.  At 15:15:00 every day store PREV_VOL_PCR_DAY for each index.
2.  Between 09:15:00 and 09:18:00, if VOL_PCR_DAY ≥ 1.5 × PREV_VOL_PCR_DAY against an open BUY_CE (or ≤ PREV_VOL_PCR_DAY ÷ 1.5 against an open BUY_PE), and the minimum-denominator guard passes, exit at Live Bid. Reason: OPPOSING_OPEN_FLOW.
3.  If flow is in favour by the same margin, the trade is managed normally under the 09:45 rule (no change).
A2. Path 2 (16-Condition Matrix) – Veto & Rank Booster
1.  Veto: block any CE Buy when FLOW_STATE ∈ {IGNITION_BEAR, CONFIRMED_BEAR} or a DIVERGENCE_TRAP against CE is active. Mirror for PE Buy. Reason: OPPOSING_PCR_FLOW.
2.  Booster: Conditions 1 / 5 (★★★) with CONFIRMED state in the same direction → Final Combined Rank +1 (max ★★★★).
3.  Booster: Conditions 2 / 3 / 6 / 7 (★★) with CONFIRMED state in the same direction → upgrade to ★★★ (lots_full).
4.  Conditions 4 / 8 (scalp) are never upgraded. SENSEX continues to allow only Conditions 1 & 5 (Section 6).
5.  The Futures Direction Gate (Section 6) continues to apply unchanged and is evaluated BEFORE the booster.
A3. New Path 3 – Sudden Move Entry (Indices)
1.  Trigger: FLOW_STATE = CONFIRMED_BULL (BUY_CE) or CONFIRMED_BEAR (BUY_PE), preferably preceded by a COIL in the last 30 minutes (if not preceded by COIL, lots_scalp only).
2.  Matrix compatibility: the 16-condition state must be either a same-direction Condition 1–8 or FLAT (deadband). Any opposing condition or Conditions 9–16 block Path 3 – except Condition 12 (SC/SC) which is allowed only inside the Gamma module.
3.  Futures gate: NIFTY and BANK NIFTY must pass the Futures Direction Gate. SENSEX (gate OFF) requires ign_vsr ≥ 3.0 instead of 2.5.
4.  Strike: existing tightest-spread ITM selection. Lots: lots_normal (lots_scalp if no prior COIL).
5.  Risk: SL 20% of entry premium; Target 40%; SUDDEN_MOVE_TIMEOUT – exit if premium has not gained ≥ 10% within 5 minutes of entry.
6.  Counts toward Max 3 Trades/Day, Daily Loss Limit and 14:45 cutoff. Maximum 1 Path 3 trade per index per day.
A4. Gamma Blast Module (Indices – expiry day of that index only)
Pre-conditions (all):
1.  Today = expiry date of the index's nearest series (from contract master).
2.  Time between gamma_start (11:30:00) and 14:45:00. An optional Config flag may extend to 15:00:00; default OFF.
3.  STRADDLE_DECAY ≤ 0.50 (ATM straddle has lost ≥ 50% of its 09:20 value).
4.  A COIL state recorded within the last 30 minutes.
Trigger (all, evaluated per closed 1-min bar plus tick confirmation):
1.  OTM_VSR ≥ 4.0 on the breakout side AND ATM side VSR ≥ 3.0.
2.  VOL_PCR_SHIFT ≤ 0.50 (bullish blast) or ≥ 2.00 (bearish blast).
3.  ATM writer unwinding: ATM CE OI falls ≥ 3% over the last 2 OI updates (bullish) / ATM PE OI falls ≥ 3% (bearish). If OI_STALE, use the fallback: ATM premium ≥ +25% within ≤ 3 minutes with spot ≥ 0.15% beyond the COIL range.
4.  Spot beyond COIL_HIGH / COIL_LOW and on the same side of VWAP.
5.  Futures not opposing (NIFTY, BANK NIFTY). SENSEX: OTM_VSR ≥ 5.0 and ATM Spread % ≤ 1.5% instead.
Execution & exits:
1.  Buy the ATM option of the breakout side (1 strike ITM allowed if its spread ≤ 1%). Lots: lots_scalp. Fill at Live Ask.
2.  SL 30% of entry premium. At +60% book 50% of quantity; trail the balance with a 20% trailing stop from peak premium (GAMMA_TRAIL_EXIT).
3.  Hard time stop 15 minutes after entry (GAMMA_TIME_STOP).
4.  Maximum 1 Gamma trade per index per expiry day (a 2nd only if the 1st closed in profit and Config allows). Counts toward all daily limits.
6A.7 Category B – NIFTY 50 Stocks
Stock option chains are thinner, monthly only, and more affected by block trades and news. Hence the stock logic is stricter and futures-led.
B1. Liquidity Eligibility Gate (checked at 09:20:00 and every 15 minutes)
1.  ATM CE and ATM PE each ≥ min_atm_vol_5m (default 50 lots) traded in the last 5 minutes.
2.  ATM Spread % ≤ 1.5%.
3.  Failure → FLOW_STATE = FLOW_ILLIQUID for that stock: flow layer OFF; existing Path 1 / Path 2 rules apply unchanged.
B2. Mandatory Futures-Led Confirmation
1.  Stock futures 1-minute RVOL ≥ 3.0 at IGNITION, and futures buildup LB / SC (bullish) or SB / LU (bearish) on the latest OI update.
2.  Option VOL_PCR alone NEVER triggers a stock trade.
B3. Path 1 and Path 2 for Stocks
1.  Path 1 Opening Flow Abort as in A1, applied only if the stock passes the liquidity gate by 09:18:00.
2.  Path 2 Veto and Booster as in A2, using the stock thresholds of 6A.5 and 6A.9.
B4. Path 3 – Sudden Move Entry (Stocks)
1.  All conditions of A3, using stock thresholds, PLUS:
2.  Relative strength filter: stock % move since IGNITION minus NIFTY % move over the same period ≥ +0.30% (bullish) or ≤ −0.30% (bearish). This removes moves that are only index beta.
3.  Ban & event guard: no Path 3 entry if the stock is in the F&O ban period, or is on the Config event list for the day (results, corporate action, major announcement).
4.  Maximum 1 Path 3 trade per stock per day; total Path 3 stock trades per day capped by max_path3_stocks (default 2).
B5. Stock Gamma Module
1.  Allowed only on the monthly expiry day, only for stocks passing the liquidity gate with ATM Spread % ≤ 1.0%. Default OFF in Config.
2.  Same trigger logic as A4 with OTM_VSR ≥ 5.0 and B2 futures confirmation mandatory.

1.  Exit 8 – PCR_FLOW_REVERSAL: in a CE position, FLOW_STATE becomes IGNITION_BEAR or CONFIRMED_BEAR for 2 consecutive bars → exit at Live Bid. Mirror for PE.
2.  Exit 9 – FLOW_EXHAUSTION: EXHAUSTION state in the trade direction while position is ≥ +15% → book 50% and move SL to entry + 5% on the balance.
3.  Exit 10 – SUDDEN_MOVE_TIMEOUT / GAMMA_TIME_STOP / GAMMA_TRAIL_EXIT: as defined in A3 / A4.
4.  Exits 1, 2, 5, 6 and 7 of Section 8 remain in force for every path. For Path 3 and Gamma trades, their own SL / Target values replace the 25% / 40% values.

on_bar_close(inst):

9. Risk Management Framework & Circuit Breakers
Risk management is prioritized over profit generation. The bot features layered circuit breakers designed to prevent overtrading and catastrophic drawdown:
Max 3 Trades Per Day: Raised from 1 to 3 trades per instrument per day. Once an index or stock reaches 3 completed trades, its state transitions to CLOSED and no further orders are accepted.
Daily Loss Limit (₹10,000 Default): The user specifies a maximum daily loss limit in rupees. If the aggregate net P&L (realized + unrealized) breaches this threshold, all active positions are liquidated immediately, and trading ceases for the day.
14:45:00 Hard No-Entry Cutoff: Strictly zero new trades are permitted after 14:45:00 IST to prevent holding high-theta intraday options into market close.
15:10:00 Square-Off Warning: A high-priority warning alert is broadcast across the system 5 minutes before the mandatory 15:15:00 clock square-off.
Stale Data Watchdog (> 60 Seconds): If the market data feed drops or fails to deliver ticks for > 60 seconds, a STALE_FEED alert is triggered, and new entries are blocked until feed health is restored.
Feed Disconnection & Recovery: If the WebSocket stream disconnects, orders are paused and contracts are re-verified upon reconnection.
10. 3:15 PM EOD Watchlist & BTST Engine
At 3:15 PM daily, the bot executes an automated screening algorithm across all NIFTY 50 stocks and major indices to construct the Tomorrow Watchlist.
The 5 Screening Criteria
1. Futures Buildup Confluence: Compares current futures price/OI to previous day close. LB/SC classified as Bullish; SB/LU classified as Bearish.
2. Absolute OI Delta Threshold: Absolute OI change from 09:15 to 15:15 must be at least +3% for LB/SB, or negative for SC/LU.
3. Close Location Value (CLV): CLV = (LTP - Low) / (High - Low). Must be >= 0.85 for Bullish (closing near day high) or <= 0.15 for Bearish (closing near day low).
4. Last-Hour Momentum Confluence: Between 14:15 and 15:15, both futures price and OI must move in the same direction.
5. Relative Volume (RVOL): Futures volume in the final hour must be at least 1.5x the average final-hour volume over the last 20 sessions.
Watchlist Output & Optional BTST Entry
The screener ranks candidates into ★★★★, ★★★, and ★★ tiers and outputs the Top 5 Bullish and Top 5 Bearish stocks to selected_stocks.json. If the BTST toggle is enabled in Config, the bot enters an overnight position between 15:20 and 15:25 PM for candidates ranked ★★★ or higher.
